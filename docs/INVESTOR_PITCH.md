### Three-Layer AI Architecture

```
Layer 1: Rule-Based (Real-time, $0 cost)
├── RSI oversold/overbought
├── Supertrend direction
├── ADX trend strength
└── Multi-timeframe confirmation

Layer 2: Strategic AI (Periodic, ~$0.30/month)
├── Market regime detection
├── Daily market reports
└── Post-trade analysis

Layer 3: Risk Management (Real-time, $0 cost)
├── 2% risk per trade
├── 3.0x ATR stop loss
├── Daily loss limits
└── Maximum position limits
```

**Why this architecture?**
- **Speed**: Layer 1 runs in milliseconds (no AI latency)
- **Cost**: Layer 2 uses AI strategically (~$0.30/month vs $100+/month)
- **Reliability**: Rule-based system works even if AI is down
- **Backtestability**: Rules can be optimized and validated
